Reading:
- The state CAN encode the prompt: 4.72 -> 1.14 nats/token within natural register levels, 0.015 uncapped (memorized).
- But the tuned state does not carry the prompt forward as context. The real continuation gets WORSE as the prompt fit improves:
  cold 4.597, cap1 4.789 (= no prompt, 4.804), cap3 5.380, uncapped 6.428. Tuning finds an unnatural state that replays the prompt.
- Judge delta moves only from -0.04 to +0.02..+0.05, against +0.54 for the real continuation; fluency falls with the cap.
- Warm start (free-run 256 tokens, then the prompt) is worse than cold on every model-side measure.
- Teacher-forced, a cold read of the prompt does help the model: cont 4.804 -> 4.597 (-0.21 nats). The prompt's information gets in; it's
  sampling that loses it (EVALG delta ~ 0).
Cost: 31 min, about 9 GB; it halved v460's speed (23 -> 11.5 steps/s) while running.
