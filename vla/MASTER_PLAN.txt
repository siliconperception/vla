This document describes the goal of this project and the imagined steps to achieve it.

GOAL:
We are building a single chip vision-language-action (VLA) model running at 500 frames/s (tokens/s), using a low latency CNN based model with 30M weights.
The application is embodied AI which is capable of common sense reasoning at the level of a fifth grader.
Our objective is to train a model that is capable of performing tasks e.g. food preparation.

INTERMEDIATE MILESTONES:
1. language model only (dataset?, synthetic?, distilled?)
2. vision-language model trained on image-text and video-captions
